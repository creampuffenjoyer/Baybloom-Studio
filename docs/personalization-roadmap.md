# Personalize before purchase

Baybloom customers will try a template with their own content, then purchase a specific website. A membership governs editor access and benefits; purchasing a website delivers its code. Hosted setup and ongoing live-site updates require separately defined service terms.

## Delivery phases

1. **Draft foundation:** establish a versioned, validated Harbor House draft format. Overrides leave original content intact.
2. **Pilot editor:** All eight templates preview selected home page text, brand and contact details, a color, and a hero photo in desktop and mobile viewports. Harbor House additionally supports its three-line address; names, descriptions, bed, view, features, price, guest capacity, and one primary photo for each of four rooms; breakfast page copy and menu items; and local-guide copy and six place entries. Coral Cove also supports names, descriptions, beds, views, amenities, prices, sizes, guest capacities, and one primary photo for each of four villas, plus descriptions, durations, and display prices for six experiences. Room and villa photos are saved individually after the draft is created. Both previews include the relevant pages and a simulated booking flow. Bloomfield’s dedicated home hero control replaces the center photo; its library controls cover the other photos too. Preserve original content and provide reset. Edited copy must not be represented as translated automatically.

   Coral Cove additionally supports its address, home-page travel steps and reef copy, editable or hidden guest reviews, four contact questions and answers, the display text and inclusions for three offers, and all 16 gallery photo slots with descriptions. Gallery photos are saved independently; customers may opt to reuse the snorkeler photo for the home-page reef section and five matching photos in the home-page strip. Offer edits do not alter the sample booking's fifth-night calculation. Additional content remains sample copy. The complete photo library is now editable as described below.

   Pine Ridge supports its address and stay times, seasonal text, rooms, activities, menu, journal articles, page introductions, sample booking extras, searchable interface wording and its photo library. Photo replacements are private and saved individually; a shared image changes everywhere it is used. Edited English copy is not automatically translated, and booking logic, season date windows, currency and integrations remain sample behavior. Its full signed-in browser suite still has an intermittent development-server reload; the focused editor browser test, isolated draft test and authenticated photo API test pass.

   Lumen supports its address and stay times, villas and their comparison attributes, experiences, sample booking extras, tour scene names, searchable interface wording and its photo library. Photo replacements are private and saved individually; a shared image changes everywhere it is used. Edited English copy is not automatically translated. The sample booking and virtual tour do not make reservations or connect real services.

   Isla Verde supports its address and stay times, rooms, dining highlights, event spaces, journal articles, house timeline, sample booking extras, searchable interface wording and its photo library. Photo replacements are private and saved individually; a shared image changes everywhere it is used. Edited English copy is not automatically translated; the sample booking and event inquiries do not create reservations or messages.

   Ember & Salt supports its address, menus, dishes and dietary labels, private dining spaces, restaurant story and timeline, displayed hours, featured dishes, press quotes, searchable page and form wording, and photo library. Photo replacements are private and saved individually; a shared image changes everywhere it is used.    Still Water Spa supports its address, displayed opening hours, treatments and their option prices, packages, spa membership examples, therapist profiles, editable or hidden reviews, searchable page wording including FAQs and editorial steps, and its 17-photo library.    Bloomfield Gardens supports its address and viewing hours, five venue spaces and their capacities, wedding packages, planning questions and timeline, editable or hidden reviews, searchable page wording and its photo library. Date availability and inquiries remain demonstrations; no venue is reserved and no message is sent. Some navigation and form wording remains fixed.
3. **Accounts and trial:** one draft and one normalized hero photo per account and supported template can be saved and loaded after applying migrations `20261105090000_personalization_drafts` and `20261105100000_personalization_hero_image`. Before launching the trial, enforce a server-timed one-day trial and make expired drafts viewable but read-only. Define anonymous trial abuse controls, retention, deletion notifications, and account conversion. Do not enable expiry until a working upgrade path exists.
4. **Memberships:** implement monthly and yearly plans, entitlements, billing lifecycle, and a cancellation grace period once plan prices, benefits, and retention duration are decided. Verify entitlement on the server. Protect paid purchases from subscription cancellation.
5. **Purchase and delivery:** attach an immutable draft snapshot to a specific website purchase and produce a tested source package containing that snapshot and owned assets. Confirm image permissions and ensure purchased downloads remain available after membership cancellation.
6. **Setup services:** define and sell specific setup deliverables, hosting and domain ownership, integrations, revisions, and recurring costs. Live-site updates need an explicit support workflow.
7. **Final security audit and launch gate:** after the planned features and service rehearsals are complete, review the complete customer journey for security before public launch. Cover authentication, sessions, password resets, OAuth linking and disabled accounts; authorization across drafts, photos, orders and downloads; price/coupon tampering, webhook forgery/replay and refund races; malicious uploads, request limits, archive traversal and secrets in exported packages; and dependencies, secret handling, private storage permissions, browser security headers, rate limits and sensitive logging. Include trial and membership entitlement bypasses once those features exist. Produce a prioritized findings report, fix identified vulnerabilities and add targeted regression checks. Resolve all critical and high-severity findings before launch, and record remaining risks with an owner and remediation plan. Use only the isolated test database and explicitly configured provider sandboxes for active checks; run resource-intensive checks sequentially at lower priority on the shared computer. This phase plans the audit; deployment and application database changes require separate authorization.

Before each release, test its full customer journey and the boundary between sample and operational features. Apply security controls during implementation and address discovered vulnerabilities as they arise; the final audit reviews the completed system. Expand beyond Harbor House after the pilot and delivery path are validated.

The [2026-11-15 application security audit](security-audit.md) reviews the features currently implemented and records fixes and targeted regression checks. Shared draft-photo quotas cover all image write paths (100 MiB per draft, 200 MiB per account) with concurrent-write protection. Saved draft/image writes require verified email; submitted new passwords are screened for breaches, and password/Google signup share hourly limits. [Administrative MFA](admin-mfa.md) adds mandatory authenticator enrollment, single-use recovery and session checks. Compatible dependency updates reduce the remaining findings. The launch gate remains open: MFA rollout/key backup/operator recovery, trial/membership authorization when implemented, retention/multiple-account abuse policies, ingress controls and production-service rehearsals remain release work. No hosting target is selected yet; staging remains in planning. No deployment, application database migration or source-release activation was performed by the audit.

### Complete photo libraries

All eight templates now support selecting photos directly in the preview, with matching page and location dropdowns. The 338 named locations use shared controls for independent replacements, descriptions, original-image restoration, explicit reuse, queued saving and mobile editing. Existing saved photo libraries remain fallbacks. New location overrides require each template's reviewed 1.1.0 source; retained 1.0.0 purchases remain supported. See [the Coral Cove pilot](photo-editing-pilot.md), [Harbor House](photo-editing-harbor.md), and [the remaining six-template rollout](photo-editing-rollout.md) for behavior, compatibility and verification.

The legacy shared photo catalogs remain compatible with saved drafts. New replacements affect only the selected location unless the customer explicitly chooses reuse. Purchased delivery uses immutable snapshots and local photo files, subject to reviewed release activation and the prerequisites above.

## Draft format

The selected [retention proposal](storage-retention.md) is 30 days of inactivity. The aggregate storage report is read-only; last-view tracking, notices, entitlements and deletion remain unimplemented. Do not enable expiry or cleanup until the upgrade/notification requirements above are met. Local tooling now uses [workspace-local temporary files and caches](workspace-tools.md).

Version 1 in `src/lib/personalization/harbor-draft.ts` describes English content and accent-color overrides. Harbor House also supports optional fixed-room, address, breakfast, and local-guide overrides; Coral Cove supports fixed-villa and experience overrides. Earlier version-1 drafts without them remain valid. The format does not cover every template field or image. The editor and packaged site must use the same validated data and version migration policy before drafts are stored long-term. Arbitrary external URLs and browser-local file paths are not durable assets.

The pilot save endpoint stores one draft per account and template. One private hero photo is re-encoded and stored in PostgreSQL with the draft, with bounded input, pixel count, and output size. Harbor House and Coral Cove also store up to four separate normalized, owner-scoped room or villa photos, each saved independently and deleted with its draft. This is a deliberately limited pilot; expanding image coverage requires an asset storage and lifecycle design. Loading replaces the current content; saving replaces the previous saved version. It does not affect purchased packages. Deploy the personalization migrations, including `20261107090000_personalization_draft_images`, using the project's documented migration procedure before exposing the save feature, and regenerate the Prisma client during deployment. No automatic retention or deletion policy is implemented.

## Disposable database test

Create a separate empty PostgreSQL database whose name contains `_test`. Add its URL as `TEST_DATABASE_URL` in `.env.test.local` (which is ignored by Git). If the URL uses a pooled endpoint, also set `TEST_DIRECT_URL` to its direct endpoint. Run `node scripts/test-personalization-db.mjs`. The runner rejects the configured application database, applies migrations to the test database only, and runs the owner-isolation and photo integration tests. It never loads the application's URL into the test process. Do not use a branch containing customer data as a disposable test database.

After the migrations and isolated source preparation are complete, run `npm run test:photos:pilots` and `npm run test:photos:remaining` sequentially with Chrome installed. Follow each rollout document above for source preparation. Each suite uses one local Next.js server pointed only at the validated test database, creates temporary users, signs in through the real credentials endpoint, checks photo editing and account isolation, verifies purchased downloads, and removes its fixtures. These tests do not exercise the login form or deploy or migrate the application database. The historical combined `test:personalization:e2e` scenario exercises all eight draft routes but still expects the older photo controls; its UI steps require migration before it can be used as a release check again.
